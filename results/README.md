# Universe Damper Experiment — Methods Summary

All experiments share a fixed benchmark: the merged ATLI dataset (1,046 target images + 297 of Eduardo's annotated photos = 1,343 images, 7 classes) split 70/15/15 (941/203/199) by multilabel-stratified sampling with seed 42, and every condition keeps the validation and test sets byte-identical so per-class AP is directly comparable. The treatment conditions add community-sourced damper images from two Roboflow Universe datasets (wangbo/damper-o5wo3 and yolov11-tasks/damper-defect-detection, CC BY 4.0) to the training split only, after filtering through a perceptual-hash deduplication gate against the entire benchmark (leakage guard), removal of pre-augmented duplicate copies, and remapping of their labels onto the ATLI classes Defective_Damper and Normal_Damper. This yields three training conditions — baseline B (177 Defective_Damper train instances), Uw (+745 from wangbo only, 922 total), and Uto (+2,067 from both sets, 2,244 total) — plus a control condition Ufull in which all data is re-stratified together to measure in-domain performance on the Universe imagery itself.

Each condition is trained with YOLOv8n and YOLO11n using a two-stage transfer-learning recipe: stage 1 initializes from COCO-pretrained weights and trains with SGD (lr0 = 0.01) for 150 or 300 epochs, and stage 2 fine-tunes all layers for 100 further epochs at a reduced learning rate (lr0 = 0.00334, OneCycle lrf = 0.1535), at image size 640 and batch size 32, one run per GPU on the 8× Quadro RTX 6000 server. Validation mAP@0.5 is logged every epoch, and final models are evaluated on the held-out 199-image test split (39 Defective_Damper instances), reporting overall mAP@0.5 and per-class AP@0.5 with Defective_Damper as the primary endpoint. Initial results showed that adding both Universe sets reduced Defective_Damper test AP relative to baseline (mean 0.617 vs 0.671) despite near-perfect in-domain performance (≈0.91 in Ufull), indicating a domain/label-definition shift — partly traced to inconsistent annotation conventions (whole-damper vs partial boxes) — which the wangbo-only condition and a planned annotation-corrected subset are designed to isolate.
