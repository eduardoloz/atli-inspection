## Title banner

**Data-Centric Rare-Defect Detection for Autonomous Transmission-Line Inspection with Nano-Scale Detectors**

*Author list pending* — Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Nevada, Las Vegas

**One-sentence hook (subtitle):** A 2.6M-parameter detector, trained only on smarter uses of its own small dataset, ties a 47M-parameter transformer on a safety-critical inspection benchmark.

---

## 1. Introduction & Motivation

- The U.S. suffered 231,000+ power outages longer than one hour in 2018–2020 [1], and undetected defects in exposed transmission-line components (insulators, vibration dampers, fittings) are a contributing cause — UAV imaging with deep object detectors is the leading replacement for slow, hazardous manual patrols.
- The field, however, runs on scarce and imbalanced data: in a 2025 review of 73 studies, only 23% use public datasets and 78% flag small-object difficulty [2].
- In the ATLI benchmark (732 images, 7 classes, defect-*spot* labels) [3], **Defective Damper has 113 instances vs. 1,460 Normal Dampers (~12.9:1)** and is among the weakest classes — as damper defects are in every multi-class study we reviewed.
- This poster answers a deliberately practical question: given a fixed small dataset with one rare, safety-critical class, **what actually raises rare-class AP — more data, more resolution, more augmentation, or more model?**

*(Optional visual: one example image per class, or the class-count bar showing the 12.9:1 imbalance.)*

## 2. Objectives

1. **Fix the rare class without new labels** — find a training recipe that lifts Defective Damper AP using only the native dataset.
2. **Test every external-data route honestly** — community datasets, curation, pretraining schedules — behind a mandatory leakage gate.
3. **Benchmark nano models against heavyweight SOTA** (DINO [4], RTMDet, Dynamic R-CNN) on *identical* cross-validation folds.
4. **Audit and decontaminate the benchmark** — quantify public-dataset duplication into the evaluation split and check which conclusions survive.
5. **Export to the edge** — a Jetson-Nano-deployable engine with validated accuracy cost.
6. **Establish evaluation discipline** — multi-seed / cross-validated reporting for rare-class claims (single-split DD AP swings by >0.2 across equally valid draws).

## 3. Methods

- **Models & training:** COCO-pretrained nano YOLO [5] (v5n / v8n / v11n, 1.8–3.2M params), two-stage transfer learning [3]: 150 epochs + 100-epoch low-LR fine-tune.
- **Levers compared:** a native data-centric recipe — 1280 px training, 3× rare-class oversampling, scale-down augmentation, **zero new labels** — vs. four external-data routes (community damper datasets, curated mixing, pretraining schedules), all behind a pHash leakage gate.
- **Evaluation:** proper 5-fold cross-validation, with heavyweight SOTA (DINO [4], RTMDet, Dynamic R-CNN) trained on **identical folds**; benchmark contamination audit (249 CPLID [6] duplicates found and removed); TensorRT FP16 export for Jetson Nano.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Main result — the native recipe wins under proper 5-fold CV
`poster_fig_cv_main`

| Condition | Model | mAP@0.5 | DD AP | DD recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | v11n | 0.722 ± 0.019 | 0.743 ± 0.074 | 0.695 |
| **champ** | **v11n** | **0.785 ± 0.023** | **0.812 ± 0.080** | 0.764 |
| osall | v11n | 0.772 ± 0.024 | 0.809 ± 0.071 | **0.767** |

- Champ improves **every** model: +4.2 to +6.3 mAP points, +6.4 to +9.4 DD AP points; DD recall (the safety-critical metric) 0.61–0.70 → 0.71–0.77.
- osall is the best condition for the weakest model (v5n, 0.776) and consistently buys defect recall.

### 4.2 Nano ties heavyweight SOTA at ~18× fewer parameters
`poster_fig_frontier`

| Model | Params | mAP@0.5 | DD AP |
|---|---|---|---|
| DINO-4scale | 47M | **0.785 ± 0.032** | 0.789 ± 0.110 |
| RTMDet-Tiny | 4.8M | 0.747 ± 0.023 | 0.774 ± 0.052 |
| Dynamic R-CNN | 41M | 0.718 ± 0.023 | 0.699 ± 0.065 |
| **YOLOv11n champ (ours)** | **2.6M** | **0.785 ± 0.023** | **0.812 ± 0.080** |

- Identical folds; heavyweights run stock (default configs) — the claim is scoped to *stock* heavyweights.
- Rare class: paired per-fold Δ = +0.023 ± 0.050 → **comparable**, not a win.
- All SOTA models score *higher* under CV than on the original single split (+3.2 to +5.6): the fixed split was a pessimistic draw.

### 4.3 External data: a systematic negative result
`poster_fig_external`

- **No external-data route ever beat the baseline** on Defective Damper (baseline 0.641 ± 0.036, 11 seeds): mixing at any dose 0.58–0.65 (n=8); scale-matched curation 0.63–0.65 (n=3, recall drops to 0.51); pretrain→finetune never improved; in-domain source pretraining = **negative transfer, −7.5 mAP**.
- **Controls localize the failure:** in-distribution evaluation of the external data reaches ≈0.91 DD AP (the labels are learnable!), and a clean bird-nest harvest lifted its target class +0.136 — the damper failure is **label-style / domain mismatch**, expressed as a recall drop at held precision.
- The purely native champ recipe reached **0.699 ± 0.027** on the same split.
- **Takeaway: what transfers is label-style and curation *consistency*, not domain proximity.** Exhaust native levers before harvesting — and never harvest without a leakage gate.

### 4.4 Ablations — what moves the rare class, and what doesn't
`poster_fig_ablation`

- **All three levers are needed:** no scale-aug → −2.3 mAP; hi-res alone fixes Normal but not Defective Damper; oversample+scale at 640 px only 0.661.
- **Freezing hurts:** freeze-10 −5 mAP; freeze-20 collapses (0.448) → full fine-tuning required.
- **Rotation is harmful for axis-aligned detection** (deg10: −0.10 DD AP; deg45: −0.037 mAP via box inflation) — untested-but-standard practice in the literature. Under **OBB**, rotation inverts to helpful (deg45: DD recall 0.957).
- Architecture patches underperform data levers: P2 head, copy-paste, TTA (+0.002) all rejected; only close_mosaic=20 helped at 768 px (0.767 ± 0.003).
- **Error structure:** insulator defects are missed, not confused with normal — resolution + sampling attack the failure mode this dataset actually has.

### 4.5 Decontamination — the conclusions survive clean data
`poster_fig_perclass`

- pHash audit: **249 CPLID images inside ATLI's benchmark** (41+37 in val/test), ~50% of DVDI recycled, 32 exact PTL-AI Furnas duplicates in val/test → "adding public data" can silently train on the test set.
- Clean 797-image benchmark: baseline 0.736 ± 0.015 vs. **champ 0.784 ± 0.011** — **the margin survives decontamination**; largest per-class gain on Self-Exploded Insulator (+0.116).

### 4.6 Edge deployment
`poster_fig_deploy` (+ `poster_fig_deploy_perclass` for the all-class view, + `poster_fig_blur`)

- **768 px native retrain = deployment point:** 0.766 ± 0.009 mAP, DD 0.664 ± 0.042 — parity with 1280 px within noise at 2.8× less compute.
- **TensorRT FP16 export costs only −0.009 mAP** (8.1 MB engine, fits a 2 GB Nano budget); INT8 unavailable on Maxwell; ~18 fps *projected* (not yet measured); coverage analysis rescopes the requirement from 30 fps to 5–10 Hz.
- **Blur fragility found and fixed:** 7 px motion blur costs −0.25 mAP; blur-augmented training recovers it (0.466 → 0.678 blurred) at **zero clean-accuracy cost**.

## 5. Conclusion & Future Work

**Conclusions**
- On small, imbalanced inspection datasets, **disciplined evaluation + native data-centric training beat both external data harvesting and parameter-heavy architectures.**
- The rare-class bottleneck yields to resolution + minority oversampling + scale augmentation — no new labels, no architecture change.
- A 2.6M-parameter model ties a 47M transformer on mAP and is comparable on the rare class → the edge-deployment case is strengthened, and the result exports to an FP16 engine at −0.009 mAP.
- Evaluation hygiene is a first-class problem: single-split rare-class AP swings >0.2 across valid draws, and three public datasets duplicate into the evaluation split.

**Future work**
- **Measure** on-device throughput/memory/power (projections only, today); Orin-class hardware for full-resolution real time.
- Matched-budget application of the data-centric levers to a heavyweight baseline (are they model-agnostic?).
- Class-weighted / focal losses for the normal-vs-defective split — untried field-wide.
- Detect-then-classify heads for residual confusion; blur-robust training before flight video; **enlarge the defective-damper evaluation pool** — the single highest-value data investment.
